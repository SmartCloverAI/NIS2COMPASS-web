# Phishing Awareness

Phishing uses impersonation to persuade someone to take an unsafe action. It can arrive through a message, a phone call or a QR code. The [UK National Cyber Security Centre's guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/phishing-scams/spot-scams) explains common warning signs.

- [Recognise and respond](recognise-and-respond.md): practical advice for suspicious requests and what to do after a click or disclosure.
- [The NIS2COMPASS approach](project-approach.md): the planned sequence, participant safeguards and fair measurement.
- [Awareness and phishing metrics](../playbooks/awareness-and-phishing-metrics-guide.md): how to keep training records and simulation findings distinct.

> As of 28 October 2026, NIS2COMPASS has prepared its awareness approach. This is not a report of completed training, delivered simulations or measured improvement. General advice here does not replace workplace incident procedures or provide Romanian legal guidance.
