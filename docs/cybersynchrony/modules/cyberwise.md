# CYBERWISE

CYBERWISE focuses on cybersecurity awareness and learning. Its purpose is to help people recognize risks, practise safer behaviour, and understand their role before an incident occurs.

## What This Means For NIS2COMPASS

NIS2COMPASS uses the CYBERWISE role to publish beginner-friendly learning material and privacy-aware guidance for aggregate awareness metrics.

> Public material avoids individual risk scores, recipient lists, campaign configurations, personal performance records, and claims that a single training score proves secure behaviour.

## Selected Tool

Selection note: 28 October 2026.

TrustSENSE facilitates a team-consensus assessment of artificial intelligence trustworthiness and readiness. Participants discuss it as a group; the expected output is a group assessment. It is relevant to baseline review where applicable, including the smaller workstream only where separately authorised.

> TrustSENSE is not a phishing simulator, an individual staff-ranking tool or proof of NIS2 maturity. Its findings must remain separate from training completion and phishing outcomes. Selection does not establish deployment or completed assessment. See the [eight-tool selection and conditions](../selected-tools.md).
