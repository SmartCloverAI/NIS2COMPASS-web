# CYRESCUE

CYRESCUE focuses on preparedness, incident response, and resilience. In plain language, it asks whether an organization can discover weaknesses safely, practise its response, learn from the exercise, and recover without turning a controlled test into a new risk.

## What This Means For NIS2COMPASS

NIS2COMPASS uses the CYRESCUE role to organize public guidance for authorized validation, Rules of Engagement, reviewable test records, remediation evidence, and lessons learned. Public material describes the method and safety boundaries.

> Public material does not publish targets, findings, payloads, credentials, private architecture, or step-by-step attack instructions.

## Selected Tools

Selection note: 28 October 2026.

Cyber Security Posture Assessment (CSPA) helps an analyst relate an authorised equipment and software inventory to defensive techniques. It supports baseline review, with human checking of inventory matches and context. Purple Team Assessment (PTA) organises, scores and reports authorised assessment evidence; its expected outputs are reviewed records and reports.

> CSPA does not connect to hosts or need their credentials, and an automatic export is not promised. PTA does not execute attacks or issue automatic security certification. Selection does not establish deployment or authorise testing. See the [eight-tool selection and conditions](../selected-tools.md).

## Independent Open-Source Reference

<!-- tool-ref:apache-caldera -->

**[Apache Caldera](https://caldera.apache.org/)** is an Apache-2.0 adversary-emulation platform that security teams can study for controlled defensive validation. It is listed here as an independent upstream research reference relevant to CYRESCUE's public role.

> NIS2COMPASS does not present Apache Caldera as a CYberSynchrony-supplied component, a completed integration, or a tool approved for unrestricted use. Because adversary emulation is dual-use, any real activity requires written authorization, an isolated scope, explicit stop conditions, responsible operators, and review of the resulting evidence.
